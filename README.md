# Mandi — Smart Kirana Inventory & B2B Ordering

Mandi is a smart inventory management and B2B ordering app designed to help kirana shop owners manage stock, identify replenishment needs, discover suppliers, and track orders through a simple, mobile-first interface.

Prototype Link: https://ayanvsingh.github.io/mandi-smart-inventory/

## How Mandi Works

### 1. Inventory Monitoring
Mandi provides a centralized dashboard of the products available in a store. Shop owners can view product categories, individual stock levels, SKU quantities, and overall inventory status. Products can be searched in the repository and added or updated through scanning or manual entry.

### 2. Smart Low-Stock Alerts
The app monitors stock levels against a defined inventory baseline. When a product reaches or falls below the 30% reorder threshold, Mandi highlights it as requiring attention. Products are categorized by stock criticality, helping shop owners prioritize essential replenishment and reduce the risk of running out of stock.

### 3. Simplified Reordering
The Reorder section brings critical and low-stock products together in one place. Shop owners can review the affected products, adjust order quantities, explore supplier options, and initiate a replenishment order based on their requirements.

### 4. City-Wise Supplier Discovery
Mandi includes a city-based vendor directory that allows shop owners to select a location and browse relevant supplier listings. Vendor cards provide available business details, addresses, product categories, and contact information to help shop owners identify potential sources for replenishment.

### 5. Order Tracking
After initiating an order, shop owners can view its progress through a dedicated tracking interface. The screen displays the order placement date, status milestones, supplier details, and expected delivery date, providing a clearer view of the replenishment process.

### 6. Accessible and Personalized Experience
Mandi is designed for small retailers who need a straightforward interface that works well on mobile devices. It includes language selection for English and major Indian languages, light and dark themes, notification preferences, and configurable reorder settings.

## Core Value Proposition

Mandi brings inventory visibility, stock alerts, supplier discovery, and replenishment tracking into one interface. By helping shop owners identify what needs restocking and organize the next steps, the app aims to make inventory management simpler and more structured.
